How is the estimate calculated?

  • Annual income = approved daily rate for the district × 365 × occupancy.
  • We show four occupancy scenarios: 55%, 60%, 65% and 70%.
  • Values are gross estimates before expenses, fees and taxes, and are not a guaranteed return.
